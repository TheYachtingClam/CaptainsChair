## Rulings and open questions

- "Every Ship with Alien" is read as your own Ships only, since it is not an attack.
- The Encounter is taken into hand and then discarded, so it ends up owned, in the Discard pile.
- Rulebook example (p. 13): 1 Glory placed on Universal Translator in the Market.
- Ruling (answered 2026-10-08): a Wildcard counts as an Alien for the second PLAY only when it is needed as one of the three. A Wildcard used that way is dismissed if it is a Ship or beamed, like any other Alien. A Wildcard that was not needed is kept. With more Wildcards than needed, you choose which count.

## Tests

- Given only 2 Alien cards in play, then the second PLAY only logs this card.
- Given 2 Alien cards and a beamed Wildcard in play, when the second PLAY resolves, then the Wildcard counts as the third Alien and is dismissed.
- Given 3 Alien cards and a beamed Wildcard in play, when the second PLAY resolves, then the Wildcard is kept.
