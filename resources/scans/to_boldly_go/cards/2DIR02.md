# Reinforce

## Printed text

> **PLAY:** Take a card from your Reinforcement pile.  
> **PLAY:** If your Reinforcement pile is empty, draw a card, then log this card.

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Take a card from your Reinforcement pile.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Take a card of your choice from your Reinforcement pile into hand.
- **Actions used:** `TAKE_FROM_REINFORCEMENT`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** REQ-CAMP-21

### 2. PLAY

- **Printed:** If your Reinforcement pile is empty, draw a card, then log this card.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** your Reinforcement pile is empty
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Draw a card.
  2. Log this card.
- **Actions used:** `DRAW`, `LOG`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** REQ-CAMP-21

## Scoring

None.

## Solo

Used only in the Five-Year Mission campaign, shuffled into the human's starting deck when the Reinforcement pile has a card (REQ-CAMP-21).
