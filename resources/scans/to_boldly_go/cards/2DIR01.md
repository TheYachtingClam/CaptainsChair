# Time Is Running Out

## Printed text

> **SURPRISE** (Bot only): Gain 2 [Glory]. Put the top card of the Supplement deck on top of the Bot deck. Junk the top card of the Incident deck. **You draw a card and discard a card.**

## Operations

### 1. SURPRISE

- **Printed:** Gain 2 [Glory]. Put the top card of the Supplement deck on top of the Bot deck. Junk the top card of the Incident deck. You draw a card and discard a card.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. The Bot gains 2 Glory.
  2. Put the top card of the Supplement deck on top of the Bot deck.
  3. Move the top card of the Incident deck to the Junk.
  4. The human draws a card and then discards a card.
- **Actions used:** `GAIN_RESOURCE`, `PUT`, `JUNK`, `DRAW`, `DISCARD`, `FORCE`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** REQ-SOLO-87, REQ-SOLO-130

## Scoring

None.

## Solo

Used only in the Ticking Clock solo challenge: shuffled into the Bot's Reserve cards when building the Supplement deck (REQ-SOLO-130). The Bot resolves its SURPRISE operation.

## Rulings and open questions

- Junking the top Incident shrinks the Incident deck, which brings the Burn closer; that is the point of the challenge.
- Uses JUNK with the Incident deck as source and PUT from the Supplement deck to the Bot deck.
- The human chooses which card to discard, so the step uses FORCE (KW-FORCE); it is not an attack.
