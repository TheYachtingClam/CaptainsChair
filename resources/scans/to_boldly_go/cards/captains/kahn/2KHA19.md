# To the Last, I Will Grapple with Thee!

## Printed text

> [Action] **ATTACK PLAY:** Reveal a card and put it on the top of your deck to put this card in your opponent's Discard pile. If the revealed card shares a non-Human trait with your opponent's Captain, gain 1 [Glory] and they *may* draw a card.  
> **SURPRISE** (Bot only): Discard the top card of the Bot deck. **Put this card in your Discard pile.** **Draw a card.**

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Reveal a card and put it on the top of your deck to put this card in your opponent's Discard pile. If the revealed card shares a non-Human trait with your opponent's Captain, gain 1 [Glory] and they may draw a card.
- **Action cost:** yes
- **Attack:** yes
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** reveal a card from hand and put it on top of your Draw deck
- **Effect:**
  1. Put this Incident in the opponent's Discard pile (attack part).
  2. If the revealed card shares a trait other than Human with the opponent's Captain, gain 1 Glory, and the opponent may draw a card.
- **Actions used:** `REVEAL`, `PUT`, `ATTACK`, `GAIN_RESOURCE`, `DRAW`
- **Undoable:** no

### 2. SURPRISE

- **Printed:** Discard the top card of the Bot deck. Put this card in your Discard pile. Draw a card.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Discard the top card of the Bot deck.
  2. Put this Incident in the human's Discard pile (attack part).
  3. The human draws a card.
- **Actions used:** `DISCARD`, `ATTACK`, `PUT`, `DRAW`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** REQ-SOLO-87

## Scoring

- Printed VP: -2.

## Solo

The Bot resolves the SURPRISE operation (REQ-SOLO-87).

## Rulings and open questions

- Khan has three copies of this effect under different names (2KHA19 to 2KHA21).
- Putting the Incident in the opponent's Discard pile makes it theirs, so it counts toward their Burn total and scores -2 for them.
