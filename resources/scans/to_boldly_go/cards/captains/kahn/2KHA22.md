# Two Dimensional Thinking

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** Draw a card from your Discard pile. Your opponent *may* warp a Ship. If you have no Augment in play, you *may* free play an Incident. Return this card.  
> **SURPRISE** (Bot only): **You may warp a Ship.** If Bot has Augment in play, return this card; otherwise continue resolution.

## Scoring

- Printed VP: -2.

## Solo

The Bot resolves the SURPRISE operation (REQ-SOLO-87).

## Rulings and open questions

- Starts shuffled into the common Incident deck at setup (REQ-PS-11), so either player may take it.
- The opponent chooses whether to warp, which Ship and where, so the PLAY declares FORCE. A Ship that cannot be warped (S.S. Botany Bay) is not offered.
- SURPRISE ruling: the Bot's Captain counts as in play, so the Khan Bot always returns this card. Another Bot that took it from the Incident deck continues to its Incident row unless it has an Augment in play.

## Tests

- When its play resolves, then: Take a card from your Discard pile. The opponent may warp one of their Ships. If you have no Augment in play, you may free play an Incident. Return this card.
- When its surprise resolves, then: The human may warp one of their Ships. If the Bot has an Augment in play, return this card; otherwise continue resolution (REQ-SOLO-120).
