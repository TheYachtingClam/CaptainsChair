# Stel

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** Send an [Away Team] to a controlled Location. You *may* beam a card to a deployed Ship.  
> **ATTACK PLAY:** Force your opponent to dismiss a Romulan/Andorian. If they do, gain 1 [Glory]. You *may* log a card from your hand or Discard pile.  
> **ACTIVATION:** You *may* exhaust a deployed Ship to recall a card beamed to it. You *may* free play an Incident.

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Send an [Away Team] to a controlled Location. You may beam a card to a deployed Ship.
- **Action cost:** yes
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Send an Away Team to one of your controlled Locations.
  2. You may beam a card from hand to one of your deployed Ships.
- **Actions used:** `SEND_AWAY_TEAM`, `BEAM`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** KW-SEND, KW-BEAM

### 2. PLAY

- **Printed:** Force your opponent to dismiss a Romulan/Andorian. If they do, gain 1 [Glory]. You may log a card from your hand or Discard pile.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** yes
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. The opponent dismisses one of their in-play cards with Romulan or Andorian, choosing which (attack part).
  2. If they did, gain 1 Glory.
  3. You may log a card from your hand or Discard pile.
- **Choices:** The opponent chooses what to dismiss; the active player chooses what to log.
- **Actions used:** `ATTACK`, `FORCE`, `DISMISS`, `GAIN_RESOURCE`, `LOG`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** KW-ATK, KW-FORCE, KW-DSM, KW-LOG

### 3. ACTIVATION

- **Printed:** You may exhaust a deployed Ship to recall a card beamed to it. You may free play an Incident.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. You may exhaust one of your deployed Ships to recall a card beamed to it.
  2. You may free play an Incident.
- **Actions used:** `EXHAUST`, `RECALL`, `FREE_PLAY`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** KW-EXH, KW-REC, KW-FREE

## Scoring

None.

## Solo

Against the Bot, the attack PLAY targets the Bot's in-play cards; the Bot picks the most recently deployed Ship if a choice is needed (REQ-SOLO-166). Otherwise the Bot resolves this card through its Automated Command cards; in the rulebook example the Soval Bot matches its Attack row (solo p. 13).

## Rulings and open questions

- A Wildcard card cannot be forced to count as Romulan or Andorian (REQ-TR-05).

## Tests

- Given the opponent has an Andorian in play, when the attack PLAY resolves, then they dismiss it and the active player gains 1 Glory.
- Given the opponent has neither trait, then no Glory is gained but the player may still log a card.
