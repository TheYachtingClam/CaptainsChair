## Solo

The Bot ignores the printed text and resolves this card through its Automated Command cards, by trait and then suit (REQ-SOLO-82).

## Rulings and open questions

- The swap uses the SWAP_JUNK_WITH_MARKET action.
- A Market card with resource tokens cannot be swapped, matching the junking rule (KW-JUNK-02).
- Only suits that have a Market slot can be swapped: Person, Cargo, Ship, Ally.
