# Mount Seleya

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** Take control of this location.  
> **CONTROL:** Draw a card for each Vulcan in your Log.  
> **ACTIVATION:** Discard a Person to find any card.  
> **ENDGAME:** Score 1 [VP] for each Telepath you have in play (including your Captain).  
> **DEV. COST:** [Dilithium] x2, and have 1+ Vulcan logged.

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Take control of this location.
- **Action cost:** yes
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Move this card to your Location Area.
  2. Resolve its CONTROL operation.
- **Actions used:** `TAKE_CONTROL`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** KW-TC-02

### 2. CONTROL

- **Printed:** Draw a card for each Vulcan in your Log.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Count cards with Vulcan in your Captain's Log.
  2. Draw that many cards.
- **Actions used:** `DRAW`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** KW-TC, KW-LOG

### 3. ACTIVATION

- **Printed:** Discard a Person to find any card.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** discard a Person from your hand
- **Effect:**
  1. Find any card.
- **Choices:** Owner picks the Person to discard and the card to find.
- **Actions used:** `DISCARD`, `FIND`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** REQ-AS-20, KW-DIS, KW-FIND

### 4. ENDGAME

- **Printed:** Score 1 [VP] for each Telepath you have in play (including your Captain).
- **Effect:**
  1. Count cards with Telepath you have in play, including your Captain and beamed cards.
  2. Score 1 VP each.
- **Rules:** REQ-FS-03, KW-INPLAY

### 5. DEVELOPMENT COST

- **Printed:** [Dilithium] x2, and have 1+ Vulcan logged.
- **Effect:**
  1. Spend 2 Dilithium.
  2. Precondition: at least one card with Vulcan in your Log.
- **Rules:** KW-ENDEV

## Scoring

- ENDGAME only counts if Mount Seleya is in play at game end; otherwise it scores nothing (REQ-FS-10).
