# Infinite Diversity in Infinite Combinations

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** You *may* remove an [Away Team] from a controlled Location, log a non-Vulcan Person/Cargo/Ally from your Staging Area, and dismiss a deployed Ship. If you do all three, look at the top 2 Encounter. Take one of them and put it on the top of your deck, and return the other to the bottom of its deck.

## Solo

The Bot ignores the printed text and resolves this card through its Automated Command cards, by trait and then suit (REQ-SOLO-82).

## Rulings and open questions

- Each of the three parts is optional, but the Encounter reward needs all three.
- The taken Encounter goes on top of the deck, not into hand, overriding the usual rule for taken cards.
- Rulebook example (p. 23): removing an Away Team, dismissing Ti'Mur and logging Gral takes Gomtuu onto the deck.
- In the Soval Bot, a card with Path of Surak that would be logged from the top of the Bot deck is discarded instead (solo p. 11).

## Tests

- Given all three parts are done, then 2 Encounters are looked at and one goes on top of the Draw deck.
- Given only two parts are done, then no Encounter is taken.
