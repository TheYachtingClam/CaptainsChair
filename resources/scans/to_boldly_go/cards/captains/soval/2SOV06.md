## Rulings and open questions

- The opponent's choice to return an Incident is theirs; it is not an attack.
- Each player returns at most one Incident.
- PASSIVE operations never work from the Staging Area or while beamed (REQ-AS-21). T'Pau's two Variable Skill icons count as Any Skill only while she is on duty, which is why her PLAY lets her promote herself.
