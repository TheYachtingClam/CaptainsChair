# Vulcan Science Directorate

Soval's Status card. Starts in play above the Crew board (REQ-PS-05).

## Printed text

> **REACTION:** After putting an Anomaly or a Scientist into play, gain 2 [Dilithium] or 1 [Glory].  
> **PASSIVE:** After putting a Time Travel into play, log this card.

## Operations

### 1. REACTION

- **Printed:** After putting an Anomaly or a Scientist into play, gain 2 [Dilithium] or 1 [Glory].
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Trigger:** You put a card with Anomaly or Scientist into play (KW-PIP).
- **Effect:**
  1. Choose: gain 2 Dilithium, or gain 1 Glory.
- **Choices:** Owner chooses whether to react, and which reward.
- **Actions used:** `GAIN_RESOURCE`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** REQ-AS-22, KW-PIP, KW-REA, KW-GRES

### 2. PASSIVE

- **Printed:** After putting a Time Travel into play, log this card.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Trigger:** You put a card with Time Travel into play.
- **Effect:**
  1. Log Vulcan Science Directorate.
- **Actions used:** `LOG`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** REQ-AS-21, KW-PIP, KW-LOG

## Scoring

None.

## Solo

Status cards are returned to the box when Soval is played by the Bot (REQ-SOLO-26).

## Rulings and open questions

- The Reaction exhausts this card, so it works once per turn unless refreshed (REQ-AS-22). It can trigger on the opponent's turn only if you put a card into play then.
- The Passive is mandatory and fires even while the card is exhausted (REQ-AS-21).
- A card with both Scientist and Anomaly triggers the Reaction once.
- Rulebook example (p. 23): beaming Kaelon II Science Ministry puts a Scientist into play and triggers this Reaction.

## Tests

- Given the card is ready, when the owner beams a Scientist, then they may exhaust it to gain 1 Glory.
- Given the card is exhausted, when a Time Travel card is put into play, then the card is logged anyway.
- Given a card with both Scientist and Anomaly is played, then only one Reaction is offered.
