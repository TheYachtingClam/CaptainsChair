# Jonathan Archer

Archer's Captain card. Starts with 2 Away Teams; the other 4 are kept nearby, out of play (REQ-CD-ARC-01).

## Printed text

> **RESUPPLY:** If you have a deployed Ship and 3+ resources at *Earth*, you *may* exhaust this card to gain 3 resources from *Earth*.  
> **ENDGAME:** Score 1 [VP] for every 3 unique traits you have in play.

## Operations

### 1. RESUPPLY

- **Printed:** If you have a deployed Ship and 3+ resources at Earth, you may exhaust this card to gain 3 resources from Earth.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. If one of your deployed Ships is warped to Earth and Earth holds 3 or more resources, you may exhaust Archer.
  2. If you do, take 3 resources of your choice from Earth into your pool.
- **Actions used:** `EXHAUST`, `GAIN_RESOURCE`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** KW-GRES-03

### 2. ENDGAME

- **Printed:** Score 1 [VP] for every 3 unique traits you have in play.
- **Effect:**
  1. Count the different traits among your in-play cards and score 1 VP per full 3. Wildcard traits count as one unique trait together (REQ-FS-02 component 3).

## Scoring

None.

## Solo

The Bot ignores the printed text and resolves this card through its Automated Command cards, by trait and then suit (REQ-SOLO-82).

## Rulings and open questions

- Earth (2ARC02) is Archer's Status card; resources on it are not the player's until an effect gains them (REQ-EXP-50).
- "A deployed Ship ... at Earth": the Ship must be warped to Earth (Earth's PASSIVE lets Ships warp there), not just deployed somewhere. There is no comma after "Ship", so "at Earth" applies to the Ship as well as to the resources (ruling confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-27, from bug report 7aef2c07).
- The 4 Away Teams kept aside join the game only through effects such as Commander Shran's and Ambassador Soval's SPECIAL, using ADD_AWAY_TEAM.

## Tests

- When its resupply resolves with a deployed Ship warped to Earth and 3+ resources on Earth: you may exhaust Archer; if you do, take 3 resources of your choice from Earth into your pool.
- With your only deployed Ship warped to a neutral Location (not Earth), nothing is offered, however many resources Earth holds.
