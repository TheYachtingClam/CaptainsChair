# Earth

Archer's Status card. Starts in play above the Crew board. Cards are beamed here and resources collect here.

## Printed text

> **CLEAN-UP:** You *may* beam a non-Directive card here from your hand or Staging Area. If it shares no traits with your Captain, gain 1 [Influence] and place 1 [Glory] here.  
> **REACTION:** After discarding a Directive during your Action Step, place 1 [Dilithium]/[Latinum] here.  
> **PASSIVE:** Ship can warp here. Cards beamed here cannot be recalled or dismissed, not even when contributing to a Mission.

## Operations

### 1. CLEAN-UP

- **Printed:** You may beam a non-Directive card here from your hand or Staging Area. If it shares no traits with your Captain, gain 1 [Influence] and place 1 [Glory] here.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. You may beam a non-Directive card from hand or Staging Area to Earth.
  2. If it shares no traits with Jonathan Archer, gain 1 Influence and place 1 Glory on Earth.
- **Actions used:** `BEAM`, `GAIN_SPECIALTY`, `PLACE_RESOURCES`
- **Undoable:** yes

### 2. REACTION

- **Printed:** After discarding a Directive during your Action Step, place 1 [Dilithium]/[Latinum] here.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Trigger:** You discard a Directive during your Action Step.
- **Effect:**
  1. Place 1 Dilithium or 1 Latinum from the supply on Earth.
- **Actions used:** `PLACE_RESOURCES`
- **Undoable:** yes

### 3. PASSIVE

- **Printed:** Ship can warp here. Cards beamed here cannot be recalled or dismissed, not even when contributing to a Mission.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Modifier: Earth counts as a Location your Ships may warp to.
  2. Modifier: cards beamed to Earth cannot be recalled or dismissed, and are not dismissed after helping complete a mission (overrides REQ-MS-06).

## Rulings and open questions

- Earth is not a Location card; the Passive only lets Ships warp to it. Ships at Earth are not at a Location for securing or Away Team rules.
- Beaming from the Staging Area is allowed because the card names that source (KW-BEAM-01a).
