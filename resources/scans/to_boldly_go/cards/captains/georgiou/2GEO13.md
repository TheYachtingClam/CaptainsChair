## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Requires [Military] > [Research]: Dismiss a Duty Officer. Force your opponent to either: log (one of) their Duty Officer(s) OR dismiss (one of) their Duty Officer(s) and you gain 3 [Glory].
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** yes
- **Requires:** your Military track is higher than your Research track
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. If you have a Duty Officer, you must dismiss one of your own Duty Officers. If you have none, skip this step.

- **Actions used:** `DISMISS`, `ATTACK`, `FORCE`, `LOG`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** KW-FORCE-03, KW-ATK, KW-PROM-05

## Rulings and open questions

- "Dismiss a Duty Officer" targets your own Duty Officer. It is mandatory if you have one, but it is not a cost: with no Duty Officer you skip it and the attack still resolves.
- If the opponent has no Duty Officer, they cannot resolve either option and ignore it (KW-FORCE-03).
- Ruling (confirmed 2026-10-08): in Cadet Training the virtual opponent logs its one Duty Officer (REQ-CTM-12), so you gain no Glory.
- Mission "Call in the Reinforcements" needs Burnham on duty; completing it can dismiss her (board scan).
