# Vulcan Science Academy

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** Take control of this location.  
> **CONTROL:** You *may* gain a Person. If the gained card is Vulcan, gain 2 [Dilithium]. If the gained card is Scientist, gain 1 [Glory]. If the gained card has both traits, gain both rewards.  
> **ACTIVATION:** If you have an [Away Team] here, draw a card. If you have 3+ total [Away Team] on 1 or more controlled Location, draw a card.
