## Rulings and open questions

- Stardate cards are never owned, shuffled or scored. They form an ordered pile, lowest number on top (REQ-CS-11).
- In solo play the inactive player is usually the human, since the Bot empties cards on its turn; a card the human empties goes to the Bot's Staging Area and resolves in the Bot's Clean-up (REQ-SOLO-57).
