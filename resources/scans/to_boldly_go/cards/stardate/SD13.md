# Stardate Solo vs Lieutenant Bot 1

Stardate card 1 of 5 for Solo vs Lieutenant Bot.

## Printed text

> **When emptied:** Put this card in the inactive player's Staging Area.  
> **Stardate Resolution:** Wipe the Market (removing all tokens).

## Operations

### 1. WHEN EMPTIED

- **Printed:** Put this card in the inactive player's Staging Area.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Move this card to the inactive player's Staging Area.
  2. Fill the next Stardate card with its printed Glory.
- **Rules:** REQ-SD-02

### 2. STARDATE RESOLUTION

- **Printed:** Wipe the Market (removing all tokens).
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Resolve during the Stardate Resolution substep of the holder's next Clean-up Step.
  2. Return all tokens on Market cards to the supply.
  3. Junk all four Market cards and refill the Market.
  4. Return this card to the box.
- **Actions used:** `JUNK`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** REQ-SD-11

## Scoring

None.

## Solo

Used only in the mode printed at the top.

## Rulings and open questions

- Stardate cards are never owned, shuffled or scored. They form an ordered pile, lowest number on top (REQ-CS-11).
- In solo play the inactive player is usually the human, since the Bot empties cards on its turn; a card the human empties goes to the Bot's Staging Area and resolves in the Bot's Clean-up (REQ-SOLO-57).

## Tests

- Given this is the top Stardate card, when it becomes topmost, then 5 Glory are placed on it.
- Given this is the top Stardate card in a solo game, then the Bot draws 2 cards in its Action Step.
