# Stardate Solo Cadet Practice 1

Stardate card 1 of 2 for Cadet Training.

## Printed text

> **When emptied:** Put this card in your Staging Area.  
> **Stardate Resolution:** Wipe the Market (removing all tokens) and any neutral Location with no tokens present.  
> *Only take tokens from here during Clean-up step!*

## Operations

### 1. WHEN EMPTIED

- **Printed:** Put this card in your Staging Area.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Move this card to your own Staging Area.
  2. Fill the next Stardate card with its printed Glory from the supply.
- **Rules:** REQ-CTM-22

### 2. STARDATE RESOLUTION

- **Printed:** Wipe the Market (removing all tokens) and any neutral Location with no tokens present.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Resolve during the Stardate Resolution substep of the holder's next Clean-up Step.
  2. Return all tokens on Market cards to the supply, junk all four and refill.
  3. Remove from the game every Neutral Zone Location with no tokens and refill the Neutral Zone.
  4. Return this card to the box.
- **Actions used:** `JUNK`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** REQ-SD-11, REQ-SD-12

## Scoring

None.

## Solo

Used only in the mode printed at the top.

## Rulings and open questions

- Stardate cards are never owned, shuffled or scored. They form an ordered pile, lowest number on top (REQ-CS-11).

## Tests

- Given this is the top Stardate card, when it becomes topmost, then 5 Glory are placed on it.
