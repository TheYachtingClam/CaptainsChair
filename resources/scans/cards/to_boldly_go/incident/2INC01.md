# Dilithium Shockwave

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** Spend all your [Dilithium]. Your opponent gains 2 [Dilithium]. Return this card.  
> [Action] **PLAY:** If you have a Kelpien in play, return this card, and you *may* spend 3 [Dilithium] to gain 1 [Glory].  
> **SURPRISE** (Bot only): Gain 1 [Glory] and discard the top card of the Bot deck. You gain 2 [Dilithium]. Return this card.

## Scoring

## Solo

The Bot resolves the SURPRISE operation instead of its Automated Command cards (REQ-SOLO-87, solo rulebook example).

## Rulings and open questions

- Incidents count toward the Burn while owned (REQ-OV-23) and score their negative VP at game end.
- "Spend all your Dilithium" can be paid with zero and never forces Glory (KW-SPEND-03).
