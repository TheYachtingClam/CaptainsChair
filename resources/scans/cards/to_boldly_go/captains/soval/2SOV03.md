## Rulings and open questions

- Both conditions are checked separately, so the Activation can draw 0, 1 or 2 cards. The Away Teams on Vulcan count toward the 3+ total.
- The Activation may be used even if neither condition holds; it then only exhausts the card.
- Rulebook example (p. 22): with Muroc promoted as a second Duty Officer, this Passive allows it because Muroc is Vulcan.

## Tests

- Given 1 Away Team on Vulcan and 2 on Paan Mokar, when Vulcan activates, then 2 cards are drawn.
- Given no Away Teams on controlled Locations, then activating draws nothing.
- Given one Duty Officer, when a Vulcan Person is promoted, then both stay on duty; when a non-Vulcan Person is promoted instead, one must be dismissed.
