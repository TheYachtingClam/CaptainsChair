# Soval

Soval's Captain card. Starts in play to the right of the Crew board with 4 Away Teams on it.

## Printed text

> **ACTIVATION:** Dismiss a Human to draw 2 cards.  
> **ENDGAME:** Score 1 [VP] for each of your Person cards, excluding cards with Vulcan.

## Operations

### 1. ACTIVATION

- **Printed:** Dismiss a Human to draw 2 cards.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** dismiss one of your in-play cards with the Human trait
- **Effect:**
  1. Draw 2 cards.
- **Choices:** Owner chooses which Human to dismiss.
- **Actions used:** `DISMISS`, `DRAW`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** REQ-AS-20, REQ-AS-04, KW-DSM, KW-DRW

### 2. ENDGAME

- **Printed:** Score 1 [VP] for each of your Person cards, excluding cards with Vulcan.
- **Effect:**
  1. Count every Person card you own (hand, deck, Discard pile, play, Log; not Reserve or Development), excluding cards with the Vulcan trait.
  2. Score 1 VP per card.
- **Rules:** REQ-FS-02, REQ-FS-03, REQ-FS-11

## Scoring

- ENDGAME as above. Wildcard traits never count as Vulcan during final scoring (REQ-FS-11).

## Solo

The Bot ignores the printed text and resolves this card through its Automated Command cards, by trait and then suit (REQ-SOLO-82).

## Rulings and open questions

- "Your Person cards" means all Person cards you own at final scoring, not only those in play, matching the rulebook's scoring example (Phlox and Riva counted).
- Dismissing is a cost: with no Human in play the Activation cannot be used.
- Staging Area cards cannot be dismissed (KW-DSM-04), so the Human must be a Duty Officer, beamed card, or otherwise in play outside the Staging Area.

## Tests

- Given a beamed Human (United Earth on Seleya), when Soval activates, then United Earth is dismissed, 2 cards are drawn and Soval is exhausted.
- Given no Human in play outside the Staging Area, then the Activation is not offered.
- Given the owner holds 3 non-Vulcan Person cards and 2 Vulcan Person cards at game end, then ENDGAME scores 3.
