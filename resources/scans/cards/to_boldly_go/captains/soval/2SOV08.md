# Kir'Shara

## Printed text

> **PLAY:** If you have 7+ Vulcan in play (excluding your Captain), take an Incident to take the top Encounter card. Log this card.  
> **DEV. COST:** [Dilithium] x2, [Latinum] x1, and dismiss a Duty Officer.

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** If you have 7+ Vulcan in play (excluding your Captain), take an Incident to take the top Encounter card. Log this card.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. If you have 7 or more cards with Vulcan in play, not counting your Captain: take an Incident, then take the top Encounter card.
  2. Log Kir'Shara.
- **Actions used:** `TAKE_INCIDENT`, `TAKE_ENCOUNTER`, `LOG`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** KW-TAKE, KW-LOG

### 2. DEVELOPMENT COST

- **Printed:** [Dilithium] x2, [Latinum] x1, and dismiss a Duty Officer.
- **Effect:**
  1. Spend 2 Dilithium and 1 Latinum.
  2. Dismiss one of your own Duty Officers, even your only one.
- **Rules:** KW-ENDEV, KW-PROM-05

## Solo

The Bot ignores the printed text and resolves this card through its Automated Command cards, by trait and then suit (REQ-SOLO-82).

## Rulings and open questions

- The card can be played with fewer than 7 Vulcan; it is then just logged.
- The development cost dismisses one of your own Duty Officers, even if it is your only one. KW-PROM-05 only stops you dismissing your last Duty Officer voluntarily; a cost that requires it is allowed. With no Duty Officer, Kir'Shara cannot be enlisted.
- Kir'Shara itself is in play in the Staging Area while its operation resolves, but has no Vulcan trait.

## Tests

- Given 7 Vulcan in play besides the Captain, when played, then an Incident and the top Encounter go to hand and Kir'Shara is logged.
- Given 6, then only the logging happens.
- Given your only Duty Officer is on duty, when Kir'Shara is enlisted, that Duty Officer is dismissed.
- Given no Duty Officer, then Kir'Shara cannot be enlisted.
