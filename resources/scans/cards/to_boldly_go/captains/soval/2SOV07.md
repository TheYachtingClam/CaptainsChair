# Minister Kuvak

## Printed text

> **PLAY:** Gain 1 [Dilithium] for each card you have beamed to a Ship/Location.  
> **PASSIVE:** Increase your hand size by 1 for every non-Vulcan controlled Location you have in play (max 3).  
> **DEV. COST:** [Latinum] x2

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Gain 1 [Dilithium] for each card you have beamed to a Ship/Location.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Count your beamed cards on Ships and Locations.
  2. Gain that many Dilithium.
- **Actions used:** `GAIN_RESOURCE`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** KW-BEAM, KW-GRES

### 2. PASSIVE

- **Printed:** Increase your hand size by 1 for every non-Vulcan controlled Location you have in play (max 3).
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Modifier: hand size + min(3, number of controlled Locations without Vulcan).
- **Rules:** REQ-CU-21

- The hand-size bonus applies when drawing up in Clean-up.
