# Sub-Commander T'Pol

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** You *may* spend 2 [Dilithium] to scan for either [Research], or [Influence], or [Military]. You *may* draw a card. You *may* promote a Person from your hand or Staging Area to Duty Officer (can be this card).  
> **RESUPPLY:** Gain 1 [Dilithium] **OR** spend 1 [Dilithium] to gain 1 [Research].  
> **ACTIVATION:** Discard a Human/Engineer to draw a Cargo/Directive from your Discard pile.

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** You may spend 2 [Dilithium] to scan for either [Research], or [Influence], or [Military]. You may draw a card. You may promote a Person from your hand or Staging Area to Duty Officer (can be this card).
- **Action cost:** yes
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. You may spend 2 Dilithium to scan for a card with the chosen Skill icon: Research, Influence or Military.
  2. You may draw a card.
  3. You may promote a Person from your hand or Staging Area, including T'Pol.
- **Actions used:** `SPEND`, `SCAN_FOR`, `DRAW`, `PROMOTE`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** KW-SCAN-05, KW-PROM

### 2. RESUPPLY

- **Printed:** Gain 1 [Dilithium] OR spend 1 [Dilithium] to gain 1 [Research].
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Choose: gain 1 Dilithium; or spend 1 Dilithium to gain 1 Research.
- **Actions used:** `GAIN_RESOURCE`, `SPEND`, `GAIN_SPECIALTY`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** REQ-RS-01

### 3. ACTIVATION

- **Printed:** Discard a Human/Engineer to draw a Cargo/Directive from your Discard pile.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** discard a Human or Engineer from hand
- **Effect:**
  1. Take a Cargo or Directive from your Discard pile into hand.
- **Actions used:** `DISCARD`, `DRAW_FROM_DISCARD`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** KW-DIS, KW-DRW-03

## Rulings and open questions

- The scan icons are Skill icons (tab-shaped), not Focus icons.
- RESUPPLY only triggers while T'Pol is in play at the start of your turn, which in practice means as a Duty Officer.
- The opponent's Solo Soval Bot resolves T'Pol through its Telepath row (solo p. 11).
