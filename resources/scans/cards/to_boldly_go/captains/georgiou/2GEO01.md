# Philippa Georgiou

Georgiou's Captain card. Starts in play with 5 Away Teams on it.

## Printed text

> **ACTIVATION:** Remove an [Away Team] from a Location to return an Incident.  
> **ENDGAME:** Score 1 [VP] for every 3 cards you have in play.

## Operations

### 1. ACTIVATION

- **Printed:** Remove an [Away Team] from a Location to return an Incident.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** an Incident in your hand
- **Cost:** remove one of your Away Teams from any Location (it returns to your Captain)
- **Effect:**
  1. Return an Incident from your hand to the bottom of the Incident deck.
- **Actions used:** `REMOVE_AWAY_TEAM`, `RETURN_INCIDENT`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** REQ-AS-20, KW-SEND-05, KW-RETI

### 2. ENDGAME

- **Printed:** Score 1 [VP] for every 3 cards you have in play.
- **Effect:**
  1. Count your cards in play: Captain, Status, Duty Officers, Fleet Area, Location Area and beamed cards (KW-INPLAY-01).
  2. Score 1 VP per full 3 cards.
- **Rules:** KW-INPLAY, REQ-FS-03

None.

## Rulings and open questions

- The Location may be neutral or controlled.
- The Staging Area is empty at final scoring, so it adds nothing.
- The Activation cannot be used with no Incident in hand. The engine only offers it when you have both an Away Team on a Location and an Incident in hand.

- Given 10 cards in play at game end, then ENDGAME scores 3.
