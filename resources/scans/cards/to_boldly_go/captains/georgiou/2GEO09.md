# Admiral Anderson

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** Enlist a Development and draw a card.  
> **PASSIVE:** After putting a Starfleet into play (including this card), you *may* draw a card.  
> **DEV. COST:** [Dilithium] x4

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Enlist a Development and draw a card.
- **Action cost:** yes
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. Enlist a Development, paying its cost.
  2. Draw a card, which will be the enlisted card.
- **Actions used:** `ENLIST_DEVELOPMENT`, `DRAW`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** KW-ENDEV-02

### 2. PASSIVE

- **Printed:** After putting a Starfleet into play (including this card), you may draw a card.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Trigger:** You put a Starfleet card into play, including Anderson himself.
- **Effect:**
  1. You may draw a card.
- **Actions used:** `DRAW`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** REQ-AS-21, KW-PIP

## Solo

The Bot ignores the printed text and resolves this card through its Automated Command cards, by trait and then suit (REQ-SOLO-82).

## Rulings and open questions

- PASSIVE operations never work from the Staging Area or while beamed (REQ-AS-21). The Passive works only while Anderson is a Duty Officer. Playing him does not trigger it, because he is in the Staging Area. "Including this card" applies when he is promoted straight from hand or the Discard pile, which puts him into play on duty (KW-PIP-01).
- Enlisting is allowed even with Reserves left because the card says so (KW-ENDEV-02).

## Tests

- When Anderson is played, the player enlists a Development and draws it; the Passive does not trigger.
- Given Anderson on duty, when a Starfleet card is played, the player may draw a card.
- When Anderson is promoted from hand, the Passive triggers on himself.
