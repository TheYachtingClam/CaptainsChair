## Rulings and open questions

- Jackabog's text overrides the normal beaming rule. Beaming normally takes a card from hand and never from the top of the Draw deck, unless a card says so. Here the just-gained Ship may be beamed from wherever it was placed, the top of the Draw deck or the Discard pile.
