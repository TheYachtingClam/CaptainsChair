## Printed text

> **ATTACK PLAY:** Force your opponent to dismiss (one of) their Duty Officer(s). If they do, they *may* draw a card. You *may* put a card on the top of your deck to gain 1 [Glory]. Draw a card for each Vulcan you have in play (excluding this card, max 5).

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Force your opponent to dismiss (one of) their Duty Officer(s). If they do, they may draw a card. You may put a card on the top of your deck to gain 1 [Glory]. Draw a card for each Vulcan you have in play (excluding this card, max 5).
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** yes
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. The opponent dismisses one of their Duty Officers (attack part).
  2. If they did, they may draw a card.
  3. You may put a card from hand on top of your deck to gain 1 Glory.
  4. Draw one card per other Vulcan you have in play, up to 5.
- **Actions used:** `ATTACK`, `FORCE`, `DISMISS`, `DRAW`, `PUT`, `GAIN_RESOURCE`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** KW-FORCE, KW-ATK

## Solo

Against the Bot, the Bot dismisses its Duty Officer and flips its SUITS card back. The Bot never draws; per REQ-SOLO-186 it discards its top card instead.
