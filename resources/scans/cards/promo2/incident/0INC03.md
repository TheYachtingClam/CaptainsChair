# Subspace Rhapsody

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** Put a Directive in your Staging Area (without triggering its play operation) to return this card. You *may* sing the text of its first play operation to refresh either a Duty Officer **OR** your Captain.  
> **SPECIAL:** During setup, replace a random Incident in the Incident deck with this card.

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Put a Directive in your Staging Area (without triggering its play operation) to return this card. You may sing the text of its first play operation to refresh either a Duty Officer OR your Captain.
- **Action cost:** yes
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** put a Directive from hand into your Staging Area without resolving it
- **Effect:**
  1. Return this card.
  2. You may sing the Directive's first PLAY text; if you do, refresh a Duty Officer or your Captain.
- **Actions used:** `PUT`, `RETURN_INCIDENT`, `REFRESH`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** KW-PIP

### 2. SPECIAL

- **Printed:** During setup, replace a random Incident in the Incident deck with this card.
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Effect:**
  1. At central setup, remove a random Incident from the Incident deck and shuffle this card in.
- **Rules:** REQ-CS-05

## Scoring

- Printed VP: -2.
- -2 VP if still owned at game end.

## Solo

The Bot ignores the printed text and resolves this card through its Automated Command cards, by trait and then suit (REQ-SOLO-82).

## Rulings and open questions

- Promo card (set code 0xxx/?). Included only when the host turns on promo cards at game creation; it is then shuffled into the common deck of its suit at setup (REQ-CS-20 to REQ-CS-23).
- Singing cannot be checked online. The client should show a "Did you sing it?" confirmation, which counts as singing.
- The Directive put into the Staging Area counts as put into play (KW-PIP-01).
