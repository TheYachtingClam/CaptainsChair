# Starbase 80

## Printed text

> **CONTROL:** Gain a Person/Cargo/Ship from the Junk.  
> [Action] **ACTIVATION:** Beam a Person here to junk an Incident from your hand or Discard pile. You *may* junk a card from the Market.

## Rulings and open questions

- Promo card (set code 0xxx/?). Included only when the host turns on promo cards at game creation; it is then shuffled into the common deck of its suit at setup (REQ-CS-20 to REQ-CS-23).
- Junking an Incident from hand is unusual: Incidents are normally returned, not junked. A junked Incident no longer counts toward the Burn and is out of play.
- Junking the Incident uses JUNK with your hand or Discard pile as its source, so the Market does not refill.
