## Rulings and open questions

- Second Contact expansion card: only used when the expansion is included (REQ-EXP-01).
- Starts in play with 1 of Pike's Away Teams on it (REQ-EXP-PIK-01). Its CONTROL does not resolve at setup.
- Pike has no starting deployed Ship; Starbase One's Activation is his way to promote.
- Ruling (answered 2026-10-08): the draw Activation is offered only with 2 or more Ships in play. This is not a printed requirement; it avoids exhausting Starbase One and drawing nothing.
