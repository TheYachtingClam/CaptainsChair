# Time Crystal

## Printed text

> **PLAY:** Discard a card to recall a non-Time Travel card from your Staging Area. Log a Time Travel from in play (can be this card).  
> **PLAY:** Take an Incident to duplicate a play operation of a Person or Ally from your Development pile.

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Discard a card to recall a non-Time Travel card from your Staging Area. Log a Time Travel from in play (can be this card).
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** discard a card
- **Effect:**
  1. Recall a card without Time Travel from your Staging Area.
  2. Log one of your in-play Time Travel cards, which may be Time Crystal itself.
- **Actions used:** `DISCARD`, `RECALL`, `LOG`
- **Undoable:** yes

### 2. PLAY

- **Printed:** Take an Incident to duplicate a play operation of a Person or Ally from your Development pile.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** take an Incident
- **Effect:**
  1. Resolve a PLAY operation of a Person or Ally in your Development pile as Time Crystal.
- **Actions used:** `TAKE_INCIDENT`, `DUPLICATE`
- **Undoable:** no
