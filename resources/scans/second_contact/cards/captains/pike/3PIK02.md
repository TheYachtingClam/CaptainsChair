Pike's Status card. Starts in play above the Crew board.

## Printed text

> **CLEAN-UP:** If you have no Shady/Attack in play, gain 1 [Influence]; otherwise spend 1 [Glory], if able.  
> **REACTION:** After you put a card with one or more Skill icons into play (excluding beaming), gain 1 on the Specialty track matching **one** of those icons.
