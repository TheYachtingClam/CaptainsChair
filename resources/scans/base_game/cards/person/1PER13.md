# Lursa

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** Gain 1 [Latinum]. You *may* beam this card to a Ship to send an [Away Team] to its Location. You *may* spend 1 [Dilithium] and take an Incident to gain a Ship.  
> **ACTIVATION:** Discard a card to draw a card. If the discarded card is Romulan, gain 1 [Military]. If it is Business, gain 1 [Latinum]. If it has both traits, gain both rewards.
