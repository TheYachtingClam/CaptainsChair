# Recruit

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** Put a card on the top of your deck to gain a Person.  
> [Action] **PLAY:** Take an Incident to gain an Ally.

## Operations

### 1. PLAY

- **Printed:** Put a card on the top of your deck to gain a Person.
- **Action cost:** yes
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** put a card from hand on top of your Draw deck
- **Effect:**
  1. Gain a Person.
- **Actions used:** `PUT`, `GAIN_CARD`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** KW-PUT, KW-GAIN

### 2. PLAY

- **Printed:** Take an Incident to gain an Ally.
- **Action cost:** yes
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** take an Incident
- **Effect:**
  1. Gain an Ally.
- **Actions used:** `TAKE_INCIDENT`, `GAIN_CARD`
- **Undoable:** no
- **Rules:** KW-TAKE, KW-GAIN

## Scoring

None.

## Solo

The Bot ignores the printed text and resolves this card through its Automated Command cards, by trait and then suit (REQ-SOLO-82).

## Rulings and open questions

- The gained Person may also go on top of the deck, above the card just put there (REQ-GN-07).
- Same card as 2GEO16; the two share code. Where the Core Box wording differs slightly, this spec keeps the later wording.

## Tests

- Given Recruit is the only card in hand, then the first PLAY cannot be played.
- When the second PLAY resolves, the Incident goes to hand before the Ally is gained.
