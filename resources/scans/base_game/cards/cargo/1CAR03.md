# Cloaking Device

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** Refresh up to 3 of your Ship. Draw 2 cards. Junk a card from the Market. Deploy this card.  
> **REACTION:** After deploying a Ship without Cloak, that Ship is additionally treated as Cloak for the remainder of your turn, and you *may* discard a card to send an [Away Team] to a Location, ignoring any opponent Ship.

## Operations

- **Printed:** Refresh up to 3 of your Ship. Draw 2 cards. Junk a card from the Market. Deploy this card.
- **Action cost:** yes

### 2. REACTION

- **Printed:** After deploying a Ship without Cloak, that Ship is additionally treated as Cloak for the remainder of your turn, and you may discard a card to send an [Away Team] to a Location, ignoring any opponent Ship.
- **Action cost:** no
- **Attack:** no
- **Requires:** none
- **Cost:** none
- **Trigger:** You deploy a Ship that does not have Cloak.
- **Effect:**
  1. That Ship is also treated as Cloak until the end of your turn.
  2. You may discard a card to send an Away Team to any Location, ignoring the rule about opponent Ships.
- **Actions used:** `DISCARD`, `SEND_AWAY_TEAM`
- **Undoable:** yes
- **Rules:** KW-TREAT, KW-SEND-03

## Rulings and open questions

- Marked • : a duplicate of a Core Box card, removed when combining boxes (REQ-CS-31).
- Solo rulebook example: Vadic's Splinter Group duplicating this card refreshes, draws and junks, but cannot deploy (KW-DUP-04).
- The Junk step is mandatory; if every Market card has tokens, nothing is junked.
- Core Box printing of 2CAR03, which To Boldly Go reprints (marked • there). The two are the same card and share code. Where the Core Box wording differs slightly, this spec keeps the To Boldly Go wording.
