# Forced Singularity

## Printed text

> [Action] **PLAY:** You *may* discard a card to find an Engineer/Ops. Junk a card from the Market. Spend 2 [Dilithium] to deploy this card.  
> **PASSIVE:** *Requires* [Military] 3. You *may* have an additional Person on duty.

## Rulings and open questions

- Spending 2 Dilithium is a required cost of the PLAY. Without 2 Dilithium, or Glory to substitute (KW-SPEND-02), the card cannot be played.
- Its Anomaly counts for the Xindi-Reptilian Battleship example (p. 9).
- Core Box printing of 2CAR06, which To Boldly Go reprints (marked • there). The two are the same card and share code. Where the Core Box wording differs slightly, this spec keeps the To Boldly Go wording.

## Tests

- Given 2 Dilithium, when played, the Dilithium is spent, a Market card is junked and the card deploys.
