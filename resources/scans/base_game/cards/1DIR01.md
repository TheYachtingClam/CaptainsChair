> **SPECIAL:** This card cannot be beamed or discarded by any effect except its own play operation.

> **SURPRISE** (Bot only): You must choose: take an Incident and put it and this card to the bottom of your draw deck **OR** the bot gains 2 [Glory] and discards the top card of its Supplement deck.

## Solo

## Rulings and open questions

- Solo only: an alternative or extra Ticking Clock card (REQ-SOLO-132).
- The asterisk on the VP: it scores -4 for whoever owns it at final scoring, unless it was destroyed by its own SPECIAL first.
- Since it cannot be discarded, it stays in hand at Clean-up when it is in a human's hand. Open question: whether it then counts against the hand refill.
