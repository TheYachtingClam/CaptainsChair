# Plan: "Rebner"

Rebner is complexity 8 of 10 ([requirements/15-crew-decks.md](../requirements/15-crew-decks.md) §3, KW-HELM). See [tutorials-roadmap.md](tutorials-roadmap.md).

## What the player should know at the end

- **A hand of 3.** You draw 3 at setup and refill to 3 at Clean-up, so each turn is short. That changes what to keep and what to discard.
- **Helmets and "wearing".**
  - A Helmet is beamed to a Pakled Person, who is then *wearing* it.
  - Every Pakled Person is stronger while wearing one, and the cards say "if X is wearing a Helmet".
  - A Helmet beamed to a Location or a Directive stays there; only *Rebelution* brings it back.
- ***Pakled Decree* and *Samaritan Snare*** are Ongoing Directives that work only while you have the Helmet for them.
- **The Junk.** He junks Market cards and gains from the Junk, so the Junk is a second market for him.
- **His tracks.** Research and Influence multiply by 0, even on the Basic side. Climbing them only meets requirements, so his VP comes from elsewhere.
- **Clumpships**, his Ships.

## Decisions

1. **Cadet Training, 3 guided turns.** Each turn is short with a hand of 3, so the three turns are quick. Stacked to show:
   - a Helmet put on a Person, and that Person's text changing;
   - an Ongoing Directive used with its Helmet;
   - a gain from the Junk;
   - a Clean-up refilling to 3.
2. **Assumes the Picard tutorial**, like Khan's.
3. **More is used heavily.** "Wearing" has edge cases (KW-HELM); the step says what happened, and More gives the rule.

## Steps

1. **Script** (for your review), with tips for:
   - *Rebelution*;
   - the first Helmet stuck on a Location;
   - his tracks at final scoring;
   - the Junk growing.
2. **Framework:** F2 if the Junk needs stacking (the Junk at setup is empty, so probably not). Showing which Person wears which Helmet on a phone may need a clearer beamed-card display.
3. **Check** on phone and laptop.
4. **Deploy** on your word.
