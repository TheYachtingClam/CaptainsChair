Source: Rulebook pp. 2–3.

## 5. Reference material

- **REQ-CMP-10** Two player aids (specialty icons, other icons, card types, special traits) should be reproduced as an in-app reference panel.
- **REQ-CMP-11** The score pad should be reproduced as an end-of-game score breakdown screen.
- **REQ-CMP-12** The common card list should be viewable in-app.

## 6. Card identity

- **REQ-CMP-20** Every card has a set code at the bottom (e.g. `2GEO01/23`, `2LOC05/20`). Crew cards carry a deck icon. Common cards have no deck icon.
- **REQ-CMP-21** Cards may carry markers for box combination: `•` marks a duplicate and `†` marks a replacement (see [03-central-setup.md](03-central-setup.md)).
- **REQ-CMP-22** The full card anatomy is in [12-component-anatomy.md](12-component-anatomy.md).
