# 08 – Cards, Suits, Traits and Deck Management

Source: Rulebook pp. 14–16.

## 1. Deck cycling and enlisting Reserves

- **REQ-DK-01** If a player must draw, discard, look at or otherwise use the top card of their Draw deck and it is empty, shuffle the Discard pile to form a new Draw deck.
- **REQ-DK-02 Enlist a Reserve.** Whenever the Discard pile is shuffled into a new Draw deck, put the top card of the Reserve deck **on top** of the new Draw deck. The next card drawn is that enlisted card.
- **REQ-DK-03** If the deck cycles while drawing several cards, keep drawing from the new deck. The enlisted card is drawn first.
- **REQ-DK-04** If the deck cycles several times, enlist once per cycle, within one step or across steps.

## 2. Development cards

- **REQ-DK-10** When the deck is shuffled and the Reserve deck is **empty**, the player **may** enlist a Development. They pay the development cost of any one Development card and put it on top of the new Draw deck.
- **REQ-DK-11** Development costs can include resources and extra effects, for example "Dilithium ×3, and take an Incident".
- **REQ-DK-12** If a card effect explicitly allows enlisting a Development, it may be done even when the Reserve deck is not empty.
- **REQ-DK-13** Cards taken into hand during enlisting, such as an Incident, count toward hand size immediately. This can stop the player from drawing the enlisted card.

## 3. Suits

Each card has one of eight suits in its top-left corner.

| Suit | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Person | Notable people, crew, specialists | Market suit. Table ops only as Duty Officer |
| Cargo | Gadgets, artifacts, creatures | Market suit |
| Ship | Starships, shuttles, starbases | Market suit. Can hold beamed cards |
| Ally | Minor races, civilisations, organisations | Market suit |
| Encounter | Major events and breakthroughs | Taken from the Encounter deck |
| Incident | Hurdles and distractions | Removed by playing it or by an effect that returns it |
| Location | Planets, stations | Never added to a deck. Controlled when in the Location Area |
| Directive | Utility cards such as *Recruit* and *Utilize* | A deck cannot gain more of them |

Extra suit-related labels: **Captain** and **Status** cards have no position indicator.

- **REQ-DK-20** Person, Ally, Cargo and Ship are collectively **Market cards**.
- **REQ-DK-21** Locations in the Location Area count as **controlled**. Crew-owned Locations that start controlled behave like any other Location.

## 4. Traits

- **REQ-TR-01** A card has 1–5 traits in its top-right corner. Traits have no effect of their own; other cards refer to them.
- **REQ-TR-02** A trait is "in play" if its card is in play, including beamed cards.
- **REQ-TR-03 Species traits** have octagonal icons and orange boxes, e.g. Human, Ferengi, Alien. Effects may refer to "Any Species" or "Different Species".
- **REQ-TR-04 Red special traits:**
  - **Attack**: the card has at least one ATTACK operation.
  - **Ongoing**: the card's PLAY operation deploys it to the Fleet Area even though it is not a Ship.
  - **Surprise**: relevant only in solo play against the Bot.
- **REQ-TR-05 Wildcard** (light-red box).
  - The owner may treat it as any single trait for one entire operation or one goal, whether the card is in play or not.
  - It cannot count as a trait already on the same card.
  - The owner decides, so an opponent's attack cannot force a Wildcard card to count as the demanded trait.
- **REQ-TR-06** Other traits (Weapon, Starfleet, Scientist, Time Travel, etc.) are regular traits.
- **REQ-TR-07** The engine derives trait counts dynamically. The owner's Wildcard always counts for the owner's own effects, without a prompt (ruling). The exception is *Universal Translator*: a Wildcard counts as an Alien only when it is needed as one of the three, and is then dismissed with the other Aliens.

## 5. Gaining cards from the Market

- **REQ-GN-01 Gain [suit].** Choose either the faceup Market card of that suit or the unseen top card of that suit's deck. Look at it, then place it on top of the Draw deck or in the Discard pile.
- **REQ-GN-02 Gain [trait].** Take any faceup Market card, of any suit, that has the trait. Place it on the Draw deck or in the Discard pile.
- **REQ-GN-03** If several suits are listed, choose one.
- **REQ-GN-04 Scan # of [suit].**
  - Look at the top # cards of that suit's deck.
  - Gain one of them, or the faceup Market card of that suit.
  - Place it on top of the Draw deck or in the Discard pile.
  - Put the rest on the bottom of that deck in any order.
  - If several suits are listed, choose one. The choice cannot change after looking.
- **REQ-GN-05 Scan for [trait/icon]** guarantees a matching card if one exists:
  1. If any faceup Market card has it, gain one of those.
  2. Otherwise, pick a suit and reveal cards from its deck until a match is found. Gain it, then shuffle the other revealed cards back into that deck.
  3. If none is found, pick another of the four suits and repeat.
  4. If none exists in any Market deck, gain 2 Glory as **compensation**.
  - When a list of traits is offered, the player picks one and cannot switch after looking.
- **REQ-GN-06** Taking a faceup Market card also gains any resource tokens on it, usually Glory. Then refill that Market slot from its deck. If the deck is empty, the slot stays empty.
- **REQ-GN-07 Placement.** Gained cards, including scanned cards, go on top of the Draw deck or into the Discard pile at the player's choice. Enlisted cards always go on top of the Draw deck. Taken cards always go to hand.
- Full keyword rules are in [14-keywords.md](14-keywords.md).

## 6. Taking cards

- **REQ-TK-01** Some cards are **taken** from a supply instead of gained. Taken cards go directly into hand.
- **REQ-TK-02** Encounter cards are taken from the top of the Encounter deck, usually by meeting a card's requirement (e.g. *Strange New Worlds*).
- **REQ-TK-03** Incident cards are taken as a cost of some effects or as a penalty from an opponent's Attack. Take the top card of the Incident deck unseen and add it to hand.
- **REQ-TK-04** A returned Incident, whether played or returned by an effect, goes facedown on the **bottom** of the Incident deck.
