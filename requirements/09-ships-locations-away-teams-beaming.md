# 09 – Locations, Ships, Away Teams and Beaming

Source: Rulebook pp. 18–19, 28 (Beam keyword).

## 1. Locations

- **REQ-LOC-01** Location cards represent planets and stations the player protects, surveys, conquers or otherwise influences.
- **REQ-LOC-02** Once a player **takes control** of a Location, that player may use its Skill icons and table operations.
- **REQ-LOC-03** Locations in the Neutral Zone are controlled by nobody. Their operations and icons cannot be used by either player.
- **REQ-LOC-04** Location cards are never added to a deck and are never junked. When removed they leave the game.
- **REQ-LOC-05** A Location can never be beamed to another card.

## 2. Deployed Ships

- **REQ-SH-01** Most Ship cards can be **deployed**: move the card to the Fleet Area and take its Ship token from the supply.
- **REQ-SH-02** Some non-Ship cards also have Ship tokens, e.g. the Encounter *Gomtuu*. Ongoing cards deploy to the Fleet Area without a token unless stated.

## 3. Warping Ships

- **REQ-SH-10** A deployed Ship can **warp**. Its Ship token moves to a Location the player controls or to a Location in the Neutral Zone.
- **REQ-SH-11** A Ship may warp from one Location to another.
- **REQ-SH-12** There is no limit to the number of Ship tokens at one Location.
- **REQ-SH-13** A Ship whose token is at a Location is "**at**" that Location.
- **REQ-SH-14** A deployed Ship's token starts in the Fleet Area, not at a Location, until it warps. The UI must show where each token is.

## 4. Dismissing Ships

- **REQ-SH-20** A deployed Ship can be **dismissed**:
  - Return its Ship token to the supply.
  - Move its card to the owner's Discard pile.
  - Dismiss every card beamed to it as well.

## 5. Away Teams

- **REQ-AT-01** The Captain card shows how many Away Team tokens the player has. Tokens on the Captain are the player's **personal pool** of Away Teams.
- **REQ-AT-02** Away Teams can be sent to a Location the player controls, or to a Neutral Zone Location **only if the opponent does not have more Ship tokens there** than the player.
- **REQ-AT-03** An Away Team may be placed at a Location even when the player has no Ship there.
- **REQ-AT-04** If the player has no Away Teams left and wants to place more, they may move tokens from other cards to complete the placement.
- **REQ-AT-05** Away Teams removed from cards for any other reason return to the personal pool on the Captain.
- **REQ-AT-06** The engine must validate targets for "send an Away Team" and show legal Locations.

## 6. Beaming

- **REQ-BM-01** Some cards, usually deployed Ships and some Locations, let the player **beam** cards to them.
- **REQ-BM-02** To beam, take a card from hand (unless another source is specified) and tuck it faceup under the target card. Only its suit and trait icons stay visible.
- **REQ-BM-03** A card may have any number of cards beamed to it. Some effects restrict which suit can be beamed.
- **REQ-BM-04** Beamed cards are **in play** and their suits and traits count. Their operations and Skill icons are **not** available.
- **REQ-BM-05** A card beamed to a Ship is "**on**" that Ship. A card beamed to a Location is "**at**" that Location.
- **REQ-BM-06** When the host card leaves play (dismissed, recalled, logged), the beamed cards suffer the same effect.
- **REQ-BM-07** Location cards in hand cannot be beamed to other cards.
- **REQ-BM-08** A Ship may be beamed to another Ship ("tractor-beaming").
- **REQ-BM-09** Beamed cards are public information.
- **REQ-BM-10** A beamed card that helps complete a mission is dismissed. See [10-missions-and-specialties.md](10-missions-and-specialties.md).
- **REQ-BM-11** Beamed cards can be brought back to hand only by a **recall** effect. See [14-keywords.md](14-keywords.md).

## 7. UI requirements

- **REQ-UI-SH-01** Show each Location's tokens grouped by player and type.
- **REQ-UI-SH-02** Show beamed cards as a stack under their host card, with visible trait and suit icons and expandable detail.
