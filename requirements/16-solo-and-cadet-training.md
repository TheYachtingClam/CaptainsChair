# 16 – Cadet Training Mode (Practice Solo)

Source: Rulebook p. 28. Solo play against the Bot is in [22-solo-mode.md](22-solo-mode.md).

## 1. Cadet Training Mode

A single-player practice mode for learning a deck without the Bot's rules.

### Setup

- **REQ-CTM-01** Use the two Stardate cards marked "**Cadet Training**", each showing 5 Glory, instead of the normal Stardates.
- **REQ-CTM-02** Khan has special Cadet mode rules. See [15-crew-decks.md](15-crew-decks.md).

### Rule changes

- **REQ-CTM-10** All Glory the player gains comes from the **supply**, not from the Stardate cards.
- **REQ-CTM-11** If an effect tells the player to remove Glory from a Stardate card outside their Clean-up Step, ignore it.
- **REQ-CTM-12 Virtual opponent.** It permanently has "one of everything", except Ship tokens at neutral Locations. Examples:
  - An effect "remove up to 2 opponent Away Teams to gain 1 Glory each" yields 1 Glory.
  - "Steal 3 Dilithium" steals only 1.
  - Securing a neutral Location still needs only 3 tokens, because the virtual opponent has 1 Away Team there.
- **REQ-CTM-13** When the virtual opponent would take an Incident, skip it and the player gains 1 Glory. When the player gives an Incident to the opponent, the player gains 1 Glory and returns the Incident.
- **REQ-CTM-14** If the Burn is triggered, the player **loses**.
- **REQ-CTM-15** Undo and the can't-be-undone warnings work exactly as in normal play. See [20-undo.md](20-undo.md).

### Clean-up and Stardates

- **REQ-CTM-20** During Clean-up, wipe **one** Market card of the player's choice: return its tokens and junk it. Then place 1 Glory from the Stardate card on a Market card.
- **REQ-CTM-21** Core Box Burnham variant: remove 1 Glory from the Stardate and place 1 Dilithium instead. Her Status card says 2, but Cadet mode uses 1.
- **REQ-CTM-22** The first Stardate card empties during turn 5's Clean-up.
  - Put it in the player's Staging Area, then refill the second Stardate card with 5 Glory from the supply.
  - In turn 6's Stardate Resolution, wipe the Market and every Neutral Zone Location with no player tokens.
  - Then continue normal Clean-up: wipe one card and place 1 Glory.
- **REQ-CTM-23** The second Stardate empties in turn 10's Clean-up. This ends the game after turn 11; proceed to Final Scoring.

### Rating

| Score | Message |
|---|---|
| 70+ VP | You understand how to play *Captain's Chair* |
| 100+ VP | You're getting quite proficient with this deck |
| Over 120 VP | You have done an outstanding job |
